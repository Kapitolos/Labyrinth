Four hunters. One locked maze.

Locked with
the Minotaur

The sword at the heart holds three charges. Only its bearer can wound the Minotaur, and each wound spends one. Kill a hunter to take the charges they carry. The one who lands the last blow is the one who leaves.

  • The sword lies in the center. Only one hunter can hold it, and its charges run out. Whoever holds it swings that blade up close. A bow or a staff cannot spend the charge. If it falls, it stays marked on the map until someone picks it up. Each hunter starts with one charge, and only on their first life. A fallen hunter drops loot and returns at full health.
  • Chests hide vigor, fire, and a gift for your calling. Goblins sometimes drop a charge. A dropped charge pulses, and it marks the map for a few seconds before it fades. Fire spreads when the gift says so.
  • Goblins crowd the halls. They cock their blade before they strike, and slain ones return slowly. A knight holds the shield to block, and a shield raised as the blow lands parries it.
  • Each hunt opens the next maze: stone, lava, snow, mire, dunes, and stranger ground.
  • Running spends stamina. Striking does not. The thief catches their breath fastest. The knight is slowest.
  • Twin pads stand at the ends of the maze. Step through, then the way sleeps for ten seconds.
  • The Minotaur kills quickly. It comes only for a hunter it can see, or one who has just appeared on the map. Do not stand in its path.
  • The maze is dark. You see what lies ahead, and another hunter only when they enter that light. A kill, the sword, or a wound on the horn marks them on the map for a few seconds.
  • Doors stand in the halls. Opening one squeaks. That squeak marks the map only if you are close enough to hear it.
  • A few cultists, wizards in black, haunt the corridors and throw fire. They return slowly.
  • A lever stands just outside the wing it turns. The wing turns slowly, and anyone inside turns with it.
  • Look who is inside, and whether the Minotaur is with them, before you throw it.
  • Empty seats are filled by rivals who will take the kill if you hesitate.
  • Click a calling to change it. A charged sword blow lands with weight, wounds the Minotaur, and may stun him.
